    Recommendations for next iterations
    1. Separate same-store sales from chain growth. Spui only opened in 2026 — while it's ramping up, total-chain revenue growth will look stronger than what your three established shops are actually doing. Restaurant groups typically report "same-store sales" (locations open >12 months) alongside total sales for exactly this reason.
    2. Reconcile delivery revenue consistently across locations. Only Cuyp currently isolates Ubereats as its own line; the other three appear to fold delivery into "Diversen." That hides your real channel mix and blended take-rate exposure — worth asking Lightspeed's Financial API for a consistent revenue-group breakdown per location once the connection is live.
    3. Review prime cost weekly, not just at month-end. You're already tracking it, which puts you ahead of most independents — operators who catch food/labor cost drift weekly avoid it compounding into a full month of lost margin.
    4. Add average check size and covers once transaction-level data flows in. Revenue and labor % tell you what happened; covers and average check start telling you why (traffic vs. spend-per-visit), and unlock a cleaner same-store comps calculation than revenue alone.
